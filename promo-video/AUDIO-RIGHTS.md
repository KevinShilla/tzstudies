# Music and asset rights

The two instrumentals were composed and synthesized specifically for these MyTZStudies videos by `tools/score.py`. Electric-piano tones, pads, bass, drums, transition air and UI accents are generated from oscillators and seeded noise. No third-party recordings, samples, stock music, or voice performances are used. The audio can be used with these advertisements without a stock-music attribution or clearance requirement.

The website, branding, tutor photos and exam-answer pages are real assets from the user's MyTZStudies project. Their existing ownership and permissions continue to apply. The film does not add third-party stock footage or imply an official exam-board endorsement.

Remotion is the rendering software. Its software license is separate from the original soundtrack and the exported video. Consult the current Remotion license before adopting this editable project in a larger organization: https://www.remotion.dev/license.
