Question 3 — Interest and units
3(a). How much must be lent to earn Tsh 36,000 simple interest at 5% per
annum in 6 months?
Step 1: Use t = 6/12 = 1/2 year and I = Prt/100.
Step 2: P = 100I/(rt) = (100 × 36,000)/(5 × 1/2) = 1,440,000.
Final Answer: Tsh 1,440,000.
3(b). Nyanjara bought 50 bottles of milk, each holding 300 ml, for 70 children.
Find the total litres bought.
Step 1: Total volume = 50 × 300 = 15,000 ml.
Step 2: Since 1000 ml = 1 litre, 15,000 ml = 15 litres; the number of children does not change
the volume bought.
Final Answer: 15 litres.
