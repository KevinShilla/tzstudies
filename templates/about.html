OUR STORY

A library made by a student.

MyTZStudies began with a simple need: an easier way to find Tanzania’s national exam papers. The aim is to give students, parents and teachers one useful place to prepare.

Browse and download papers freely. Create a free account to open answer keys and keep a record of the papers you have studied.
