Privacy & analytics

We use first-party analytics to understand which parts of TZStudies help students and where the website can improve. Only the website administrator can open these reports.

What the analytics records

An anonymous browser identifier, pages visited, visible time on pages, traffic source, and actions such as searching, opening an exam, starting signup, or successfully creating an account. Referrers contain a website hostname only.

Analytics does not store your name, email, IP address, password, search words, messages, or form contents. We do not use browser fingerprinting or send analytics to a third-party service. Account information is stored separately to provide login and account features.

Your choice

The anonymous visitor cookie lasts up to 90 days after your last visit. A visit ends after 30 minutes of inactivity. We honour Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. You can also turn analytics off for this browser; studying, signup and login will still work.
