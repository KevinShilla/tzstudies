# MyTZStudies promotional video pack

## Finished videos

- **MyTZStudies-Launch-1080p.mp4** — 72 seconds, landscape 1920 × 1080, 30 fps. For the homepage, YouTube, LinkedIn, and other landscape placements.
- **MyTZStudies-Social-Vertical.mp4** — 26 seconds, portrait 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. For Reels, Shorts, and vertical social placements.
- **MyTZStudies-Poster.png** — 1920 × 1080 poster for the main film.

Both MP4s contain H.264 / Rec.709 video and stereo AAC audio. The files support streaming playback before a full download completes. Short animated copy communicates the story without voiceover.

## Content and music

The footage shows the actual MyTZStudies website, its filters, a real Basic Mathematics / Form Two / 2024 worked answer, mobile pages, and the tutor directory. The real catalogue figures are 60 papers, 60 answer keys and four school levels. Answer keys are free with a MyTZStudies account. The call to action is **MyTZStudies.com**.

The original instrumentals and transition accents were synthesized specifically for these videos. No downloaded stock music, third-party audio samples, or generated voiceover are used. See `AUDIO-RIGHTS.md` for the music and font notes.

## Quality review

Both complete timelines were reviewed chronologically. Recording flashes and crowded text transitions were corrected before delivery. All 2,940 frames decode successfully; there are no unintended black frames. Integrated audio loudness is approximately −15.7 LUFS for the main film and −16.4 LUFS for the social cut. True peaks remain below −1.3 dBTP.

The editable Remotion project is saved in `../../promo-video/`, with individual main scenes, local footage, local fonts, the soundtrack generator, and review tools. Open its README for editing and export commands.
